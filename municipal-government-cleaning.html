Municipal Janitorial Cleaning

We provide ongoing cleaning and janitorial service from the front entrance to the rear trash dumpster, on a nightly or minimum weekly basis including additional services offered.

Office Space and Common Areas

  • Clean all entrance glass doors, Dust all furniture and displays.
  • Remove all trash/recyclables and line cans
  • Clean and polish elevator doors, walls, floors and tracks
  • Clean all flooring in the appropriate manner
  • Dust all desk tops, cabinets and counters
  • Clean and polish drinking fountains
  • Clean all stairways and storage areas
  • Clean all interior partitions, glass and mirrors
  • Pick up and dispose of trash throughout the buildings, parking areas and green space
  • Leave notice of irregularities
  • Turn out lights
  • Secure premises

Employee, Customer and Park Restrooms

  • Complete sanitizing of all fixtures, toilets, basins, bowls, urinals, mirrors, walls, showers and floors
  • Remove trash and reline cans
  • Refill paper and soap dispensers

Kitchen and Break Room Areas

  • Clean all counter tops, tables, chairs and sink area
  • Remove all trash and line cans
  • Clean all floors in the appropriate manner
  • Clean all walls, doors and fixtures
  • Wipe clean appliances and vending machines

Work To Be Performed On a Rotating Basis

  • Dust and clean all A/C vents
  • Clean all ceilings, baseboards, doors and ledges
  • Dust window frames, wall fixtures, signs, pictures, and blinds

Additional Services Offered To Existing Customers

  • Day Porter Service
  • Special Cleaning Projects
  • Strip And Wax Tile Floors
  • Machine Clean Hard Floors
  • Carpet Cleaning Service
  • Window Cleaning
  • Provide Customer Use Supplies (Paper Products, Hand Soap, Trash Liners)
