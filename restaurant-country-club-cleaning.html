Restaurant Janitorial Cleaning

The cleanliness and appearance of a Restaurant is the first impression that a customer sees.

Crystal Building Maintenance knows how important it is to have high standards when it comes to the cleaning. Here is what we do for our country club facility clients:

Dining Room, Restaurant, Banquet Room – front house

  • Clean entrance glass doors.
  • Dust mop / damp mop marble floors with neutral cleaner using fiberglass mops, to avoid damage to wood and marble floors
  • Vacuum carpeted areas, move tables/chairs using protective vacuum guards, to avoid damaging wood and tile baseboards
  • Wipe clean service area counters using neutral cleaner
  • Remove trash, reline cans as needed
  • Spot clean interior glass partitions
  • Dust all woodwork with non-scratch feather dusters
  • Dust ceiling areas, lights, a/c vents, window frames and wall fixtures

Kitchen, Employee Break Areas

  • Clean restrooms, as outlined in section
  • Remove trash, reline cans as needed
  • Clean all external stainless areas including counters, appliances, equipment and hoods
  • Degrease ovens, stoves, grills and broilers
  • Degrease all floor mats
  • Spot clean all wall and door areas
  • Sweep and auto scrub all floor areas with degreaser, dry mop
  • Clean all drain catches
  • Clean break area tables and chairs
  • Clean all wall areas with degreaser
  • Dust ceiling areas, lights, a/c vents, window frames and wall fixtures

Bar and Service Areas

  • Sweep and mop tile floor using neutral cleaner and fiberglass mops to avoid scratching
  • Wipe clean stone counters using neutral cleaner
  • Wipe clean foot rail
  • Wipe clean service sink and counter
  • Clean all open mirrors
  • Remove trash and line trash cans as needed
  • Degrease all floor mats
  • Dust all woodwork with non-scratch feather dusters
  • Dust ceiling areas, lights, a/c vents, window frames and wall fixtures
