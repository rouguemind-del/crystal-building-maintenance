Janitorial Cleaning and Porter Service

Condominium and homeowners associations must maintain a certain standard of their cleaning and maintenance of properties within the development.

As homeowners ourselves, the staff at Crystal Building Maintenance understands the need for superior cleaning and maintenance services. Below are the tasks we perform for general cleaning and maintenance on behalf of condominium and home owners associations.

Building Lobby, Entrance, Walkways, Social Room Areas

  • Clean all entrance glass doors
  • Clean kitchen area sink and counters
  • Remove all trash and line trash cans
  • Dust all furniture
  • Clean all floors in the appropriate manner
  • Spot clean mirrors,
  • Full clean weekly
  • Clean all elevator doors, walls, tracks and floors
  • Dust wall fixtures and window frames weekly

Pool Area - Daily Services

  • Spot sweep and mop tiled floors
  • Hose and clean pool deck area
  • Remove all trash/recyclables and line trash cans
  • Wipe pool furniture with mildew remover

Resident Floor Areas

  • Clean all interior hall glass window
  • Clean elevator doors and tracks
  • Clean all floors and walkways in the appropriate manner
  • Sweep and mop stairways, dust railings, remove cob webs
  • Clean trash room – chute door, and floor
  • Dust light and wall fixtures removing cob webs

Clubhouse Main Entrance, Lobby, Corridors

  • Clean entrance glass doors
  • Dust mop damp mop marble floors with neutral cleaner using a fiberglass mop to avoid damage to wood and marble floors
  • Wipe clean tables and stone counters using neutral cleaner
  • Clean elevator doors, floor, walls and tracks
  • Polish metal
  • Remove trash and line trash cans as needed
  • Spot clean interior glass partitions
  • Dust all woodwork with non-scratch feather dusters
  • Dust ceiling areas, lights, a/c vents, window frames and wall fixtures

Additional Services Offered To Existing Customers

  • Day Porter Service
  • Special Cleaning Projects
  • Strip And Wax Tile Floors
  • Machine Clean Hard Floors
  • Carpet Cleaning Service
  • Window Cleaning
  • Provide Customer Use Supplies (Paper Products, Hand Soap, Trash Liners)
