Religious Organizations Janitorial Cleaning

A clean and healthy environment is conducive to a positive worship environment.

Crystal Building Maintenance is very well trained in providing quality, cost-effective janitorial services. Below are the tasks we complete on an ongoing basis to ensure that the facility and its grounds are kept up to the total satisfaction of the members and staff.

Entrance, Box Office, Concession, Corridors, Office and Theaters

  • Clean all interior and exterior entrance glass doors, kick plates and frames
  • Remove trash with proper barrels and take to a designated area, line trash cans as needed
  • Spot clean spills
  • Empty and clean ashtrays and screen clean sand
  • Clean and polish drinking fountains with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Spot clean doors, walls, woodwork and light switches
  • Dust and damp mop floors with fiberglass mops and OSHA approved Neutral Cleaner
  • Vacuum carpeted areas and entrance mats
  • Spot clean small spills
  • Clean sinks and counters with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Dust all desk tops, furniture, phones, computers, cabinets, partitions and window ledges without disturbing any papers or class work
  • Spot clean all interior partition glass
  • Wipe clean white boards as requested with SU provided product
  • Wipe clean student desks with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Arrange all furniture and chairs to proper position
  • Clean Elevator doors, walls, floors, tracks and stainless
  • Spot sweep and mop stairways with a full cleaning weekly
  • Wipe lounge and lunch room tables with OSHA approved disinfectant

Restrooms

  • Sweep and mop floors with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Clean all surfaces of basins, bowl, urinals and showers with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Spot clean all walls, fixtures and partitions with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Clean and refill restroom dispensers with supplies
  • Clean mirrors and brightwork
  • Remove trash, reline cans as needed

Lounge Areas

  • Clean all counter tops, tables, chairs and sink area with OSHA approved disinfectant
  • Remove trash with proper barrels and place in a designated area, line trash cans as needed
  • Spot clean spills
  • Sweep and mop all tile floors OSHA approved Neutral Cleaner
  • Spot clean all walls, doors and fixtures
  • Wipe clean exterior of appliances and vending machines
  • Arrange all furniture and chairs to proper position

All Areas

  • Clean all counter tops, tables, chairs and sink area
  • Remove all trash and line trash cans
  • Sweep and mop all tile floors
  • Clean all walls, doors and fixtures
  • Wipe clean appliances and vending machines
